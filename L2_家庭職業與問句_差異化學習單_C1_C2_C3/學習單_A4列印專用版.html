【C2 應用組 參考答案】

1. Who is that tall boy? He is Kevin.

2. Where is Sakura from? She is from Japan.

3. How old is your grandpa? He is 72 years old.

4. Neil and Judy are husband and wife.

5. He is a famous doctor from the USA.

6. This postcard is from all over the world.

6. Where is Kevin from? He is from the USA. (注意 the USA 大寫與問號)
