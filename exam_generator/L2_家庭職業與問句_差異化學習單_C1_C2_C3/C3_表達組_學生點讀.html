📝 第一部分：國中標準段考選擇題 (70 分)
1. Mrs. Lin stays at home and takes good care of her children. She is a ________.
2. Emma Watson is famous all over the world. She is a great ________.
3. A: ________ are those young girls from? B: They are from Canada.
4. A: How old is your baby sister? B: She is only one ________ old.
5. Grandpa is 80 years old, but he ________ plays tennis every morning.
6. Walt Disney is the ________ of many famous cartoon stars, like Mickey Mouse.
7. We had a wonderful time and were very happy at the ________.
✍️ 第二部分：情境書寫與中翻英任務 (30 分)
8. 【詢問來自哪裡與回答】「Kevin 來自哪裡？他來自美國。」

💡 句型：Where is Kevin from? He is from the USA.

9. 【詢問年齡與回答】「你的妹妹幾歲？她十歲。」

💡 句型：How old is your sister? She is ten years old.

10. 【生活慣用語情境表達】形容考試或任務「非常簡單 / 小菜一碟！」

💡 運用課本片語：It's a piece of cake. (或 It is a piece of cake.)
