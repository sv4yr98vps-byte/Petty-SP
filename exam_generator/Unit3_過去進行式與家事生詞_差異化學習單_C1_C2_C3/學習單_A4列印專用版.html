一、 核心生詞選擇題（共 10 題，每題 4 分，共 40 分）
1. (   ) Mom asked me to ______ after dinner.
(A) do the dishes   (B) do the homework   (C) make the bed
2. (   ) The students ______ the classroom floor every day.
(A) sweep   (B) sleep   (C) sweet
3. (   ) Please use a clean cloth to ______ the dirty table.
(A) wipe   (B) wire   (C) wide
4. (   ) Susan is using a ______ to clean the floor.
(A) mop   (B) map   (C) mob
5. (   ) Let's ______ our wet jackets on the chairs.
(A) hang   (B) hand   (C) hard
6. (   ) Can you ______ my broken computer?
(A) fix   (B) fox   (C) mix
7. (   ) My brother ______ our pet dog twice a day.
(A) feeds   (B) feels   (C) feet
8. (   ) She accidentally ______ the glass bowl into pieces.
(A) broke   (B) bread   (C) brave
9. (   ) There are some pencils and markers in the ______.
(A) drawer   (B) driver   (C) dress
10. (   ) It is very hot here. Please open the ______.

二、 過去進行式文法選擇題（共 6 題，每題 5 分，共 30 分）
1. (   ) Kelly ______ video games when the earthquake happened.
(A) was playing    (B) were playing    (C) played    (D) plays
2. (   ) Bobbie and the baby ______ sleeping when the earthquake happened.
(A) were    (B) was    (C) are    (D) is
3. (   ) I was sleepwalking when I ______ my favorite cup.
(A) broke    (B) break    (C) was breaking    (D) am breaking
4. (   ) What ______ you doing at seven o'clock yesterday morning?
(A) were    (B) was    (C) are    (D) did
5. (   ) Mrs. Baker was working at her desk ______ the lights suddenly went out.
(A) when    (B) where    (C) which    (D) who
6. (   ) Mom was doing the dishes at half ______ twelve.
(A) past    (B) pass    (C) passed    (D) to
三、 重組句子與書寫練習（共 3 題，每題 10 分，共 30 分）
1. dishes / was / Mom / the / doing / at / seven / . （媽媽七點時正在洗碗盤。）
2. playing / when / Kelly / games / happened / was / the earthquake / . （地震發生時 Kelly 正在玩遊戲。）
3. stairs / climbing / Mr. Job / the / when / was / happened / it / . （事件發生時 Job 先生正在爬樓梯。）
