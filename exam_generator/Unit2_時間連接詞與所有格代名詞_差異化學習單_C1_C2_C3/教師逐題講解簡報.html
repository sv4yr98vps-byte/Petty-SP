SLIDE 2 / 6 · POSSESSIVE PRONOUNS

👜 所有格代名詞 (Possessive Pronouns) 全矩陣

所有格代名詞用來代替「所有格 ＋ 名詞」，避免句子重複贅字。

人稱 主格 (S) 所有格 (Adj) 所有格代名詞 (Pronoun) 例句對照
第一人稱I / wemy / ourmine / ours This sandwich is mine. (= my sandwich)
第二人稱youyouryours Is this umbrella yours? (= your umbrella)
第三人稱 (男)hehishis This car is not his. (= his car)
第三人稱 (女)sheherhers It's that girl's car. It's hers.
第三人稱 (複)theytheirtheirs These jackets are theirs. (= their jackets)
人名 / 專有名詞Jamie / boysJamie's / boys'Jamie's / the boys' The basketballs are those boys'.
🔑 記憶口訣： mine 獨特無 s，his 本身帶 s 不變，其餘 yours, hers, ours, theirs 全部以 -s 結尾！
SLIDE 3 / 6 · TIME CONJUNCTIONS

⏳ 時間連接詞 When, Before, After 句型精析

規則 1：連接詞放句首（加逗號）

• Before S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
Before Miss Turner went to work, she bought a sandwich.

• After S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
After Alan got up, he made the bed.

• When S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
When Dylan was at the airport, he called his parents.

規則 2：連接詞放句中（不加逗號）

• S2 + V2 + before + S1 + V1.
Miss Turner bought a sandwich before she went to work.

• S2 + V2 + after + S1 + V1.
Alan made the bed after he got up.

• S2 + V2 + when + S1 + V1.
Dylan called his parents when he was at the airport.
⚠️ 時態一致性提醒： 敘述過去發生的兩件事時，連接詞子句與主要子句皆使用過去式（went, bought / got, made / was, called）！
SLIDE 4 / 6 · C1 ITEM ANALYSIS

🎯 C1 基礎組 重點題目逐題解析

【第 3 題 · 電話常用語】
May I speak to Meg, please? - Yes, please _______ (hold on).
👉 解題關鍵：要求對方「在線上等候」固定用 hold on。
【第 9 題 · 所有格代名詞 mine】
This sandwich is _______ (my sandwich), not yours.
👉 解題關鍵：空格後無名詞，需用所有格代名詞 mine 代替 my sandwich。
【第 12 題 · 複數所有格 those girls'】
Whose basketballs are they? - They are _______ (those girls').
👉 解題關鍵：those girls 為複數名詞，所有格只在字尾加撇號（'），故選 those girls'。
SLIDE 5 / 6 · C2 UNCREAMBLE LOGIC

🚀 C2 應用組 組句邏輯與易錯點突破

學生在組句時最常見的兩大盲點：逗號位置 與 代名詞主格/所有格混淆。

範例 1：Before 放句首 vs. 句中
• Before Miss Turner went to work, she bought a sandwich.（句首引導副詞子句，需加逗號）
• Miss Turner bought a sandwich before she went to work.（放句中連接，不加逗號）
範例 2：多重所有格代名詞並列
• These jackets are theirs, and ours are over there.
👉 說明：theirs = their jackets；ours = our jackets，兩者皆為所有格代名詞。

🏆 C3 表達組 會考選擇與中翻英診斷精講

【會考精選題 3】時態呼應
Megan studied Chinese after she _______ (did) her math homework.
👉 診斷提示：主要子句 studied 為過去式，after 子句發生在過去，動詞需用 did。
