🎯 Lesson 1 共同學習核心鎖定 (Common Core)

📌 本課教學核心三大支柱：

  • 現在完成式結構 (Form): have / has + p.p. (過去分詞)
  • 三大功能與副詞 (Usage): 經驗 (ever / never)、持續 (for / since)、完成 (already / yet)
  • 節慶飲食與薯條歷史 (Theme): 4 大節慶食物 (Easter, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas) 與比利時薯條起源
💡 教學提點： 強調現在完成式不是單純的過去式，而是「從過去與現在有連結（經驗/持續/影響）」。

⏳ Grammar Focus 1: 現在完成式三大用法
  • 🌟 1. 人生經驗 (Experience)：常搭 ever (曾經), never (從未), before (以前), once (一次)
    👉 Have you ever eaten candy apples? Yes, I have.
  • ⏱️ 2. 持續動作 (Duration)：常搭 for + 一段時間, since + 時間點
    👉 Rick has worked here for a month. / since last year.
  • ✅ 3. 完成/尚未完成 (Completion)：常搭 already (已經), yet (尚未)
    👉 I have already eaten breakfast. / I haven't packed yet.
💡 易錯提醒： 否定句 yet 放句尾；肯定句 already 放 have 與 p.p. 之間。

⏱️ Grammar Focus 2: for vs since 判斷法則

口訣與公式：

👉 for + 一段時間長度 (數字+時間單位)：for ten years, for two hours, for a month
👉 since + 過去某個時間起點 (年份/時間點/過去式子句)：since 2000, since last year, since she was 10

實戰改寫範例：

They were friends in 2000. ➔ They have been friends since 2000.
💡 技巧點撥： 看到 since 後面若有句子，子句動詞必須用「過去式動詞 (V-ed)」！

🍟 Reading: Are French Fries Truly French?

1. The French: Gave the world hairdryer & bikini, but NOT French fries!

2. Belgium (比利時): In the late 17th century, when the Meuse River froze in winter and they couldn't catch fish, people cut potatoes into thin sticks and fried them.

3. World War I: American soldiers in Belgium tasted them and called them "French fries" because French was the main language there.

💡 跨文化素養： 英國人稱薯條為 chips (較厚)；美國人稱 French fries (較細長)。
