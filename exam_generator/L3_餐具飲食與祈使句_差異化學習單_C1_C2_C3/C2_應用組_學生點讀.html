💡 文法重點矩陣：祈使句 (Imperatives) 與提議句 (Let's / Let's not)

1. 肯定祈使句：原形動詞開頭！Be quiet. / Close your book, please. (句尾加逗號)

2. 否定祈使句：Please don't run. / Don't be late! (形容詞前面要加 be)

3. 提議句型：Let's eat beef noodles. / 否定提議：Let's not talk. (注意：否定是 Let's not！)

4. 稱呼語逗號：Jason, please open the door. / Amy, please don't sleep.
