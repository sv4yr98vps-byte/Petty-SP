【C1 基礎組 參考答案】

一、生詞辨析： 1. (A)   2. (B)   3. (C)   4. (D)   5. (A)

二、形容詞填空： 6. (A)   7. (C)   8. (B)   9. (A)   10. (B)

【C2 應用組 參考答案】

1. The frog is small and green.

2. Lions and monkeys are brown.

3. The dog is big, but it is nice.

4. Jack is tall and strong.

5. Bears are big and strong animals.

6. The forest is very big and has trees.

【C3 表達組 參考答案與評分指引】

一、選擇題： 1. (C)   2. (B)   3. (D)   4. (A)   5. (B)

二、書寫題：

6. Lions and bears are strong animals. (注意 Lions/bears 複數與 be 動詞 are)

7. Today is my grandmother's birthday. (注意 grandmother's 所有格)

8. You are a smart cookie! (課本道地讚美片語)
