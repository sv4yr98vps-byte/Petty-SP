📝 第一部分：國中標準段考選擇題 (70 分)
1. Look at the photo. The boy is the ________ of the game and holds a big cup.
2. There are many animals in the zoo, such as ________, lions, and zebras.
3. One goose is swimming in the lake, and three ________ are walking on grass.
4. A: What is your ________ fruit? B: I like sweet mangoes best.
5. Help! There is a small ________ in the kitchen box. I am so scared!
6. The house is very clean, ________ the yard outside is full of trash.
7. Look at those big ________ in the mud! Is it Bigfoot?
✍️ 第二部分：情境書寫與中翻英任務 (30 分)
8. 【中翻英任務】「獅子和熊是強壯的動物。」

💡 請注意：主詞為複數 (Lions and bears)，be 動詞用 are，句尾加句號。

9. 【中翻英任務】「今天是我祖母的生日。」

💡 請注意：首字大寫 Today，所有格 grandmother's。

10. 【慣用語情境表達】讚美同學「你真是冰雪聰明！」

💡 請運用課本片語：You are a smart cookie!
