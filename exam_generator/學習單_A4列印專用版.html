一、過去分詞 (p.p.) 選字填空 (Word Bank)
單字庫：[ eaten / baked / seen / tried / made ]
1. 🍎 Have you ever ________________ candy apples before?
2. 🥧 Jenny has never ________________ pumpkin pies on Thanksgiving.
3. ❄️ We have ________________ snow in the mountains once.
二、時間介系詞填空 (請填入 for 或 since)
💡 提示：一段持續時間 (長度) 用 for；過去的時間點/起點用 since。
1. 📚 Rick has worked in the library ________ a month.
2. 🤝 Jay and Cate have been friends ________ 2000.
3. 📺 They have watched TV ________ two hours.
三、短文閱讀理解 (Reading Comprehension)
The earliest French fries were from Belgium. When the river froze in winter and people could not catch fish, they cut potatoes into thin sticks and fried them. During World War I, American soldiers tasted them and called them "French fries".
1. Where were the earliest French fries from? ➔ ________________________
2. Why did people in Belgium fry potato sticks? ➔ Because the river ________________________.

二、長文閱讀理解 (Passage Reading Comprehension)
The French gave the world the first hairdryer and bikini, but they didn't give the world French fries. The earliest French fries were from Belgium along the Meuse River. In winter, the river froze and people could not catch fish, so they cut potatoes into sticks and fried them. During World War I, American soldiers tasted them and called them "French fries" because French was the main language there.
1. What is the reading mainly about?
　➔ ____________________________________________________________________
2. Why did American soldiers call the fried potatoes "French fries"?
　➔ ____________________________________________________________________
