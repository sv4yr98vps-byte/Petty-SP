🎯 Unit 1 共同學習核心鎖定 (Common Core)
【核心單字 1200 聚焦】
sunny (晴朗), rainy (多雨), cloudy (多雲), snowy (下雪), windy (風大), cold (寒冷), warm (溫暖), umbrella (雨傘), give (給予), send (寄送), buy (購買), make (製作)
【核心文法結構】
1. 天氣詢問： How is the weather? = What is the weather like?
2. 天氣回答： It is sunny / rainy / cloudy. / We have a lot of rain.
3. 授與動詞介系詞：
  • to (方向性)： give / send / show / teach / write
  • for (付出勞力)： buy / make / cook / get

🌤️ Slide 2: C1 基礎組 - 天氣詢問與回答
【核心例句】
A: How is the weather today? (今天天氣如何？)
B: It is sunny and warm. (天氣晴朗又溫暖。)
【常見天氣形容詞對照】
• ☀️ Sun ➔ sunny (晴朗的)
• 🌧️ Rain ➔ rainy (下雨的)
• ☁️ Cloud ➔ cloudy (多雲的)
• ❄️ Snow ➔ snowy (下雪的)
💡 教學引導： 注意 How is the weather? 後方不加 like；若用 What 則必須搭配 What is the weather like?。

🎁 Slide 3: C1 基礎組 - 授與動詞雙賓語 (to vs. for)
【句型 1：動詞 + 人 + 物】
• He gave me a gift.
• Mom bought me a new umbrella.
【句型 2：動詞 + 物 + 介系詞 (to/for) + 人】
• He gave a gift to me.
• Mom bought a new umbrella for me.
💡 記憶口訣：
• 方向傳遞用 to：give, send, show, tell, write, teach.
• 貼心付出用 for：buy, make, cook, get, bake.

🔄 Slide 4: C2 應用組 - 天氣三種表達互換
【例句：這裡夏天常下大雨】
1. 代名詞 It (形容詞)： It is very rainy here in summer.
2. 代名詞 It (動詞)： It rains a lot here in summer.
3. 人稱/地點 We/There is (名詞)： We have a lot of rain here in summer. / There is a lot of rain here in summer.
💡 常考陷阱：
• rain 作動詞時受詞是副詞 (a lot / heavily)。
• rain 作不可數名詞時搭配形容詞 (a lot of rain / heavy rain)。

⚖️ Slide 5: C2 應用組 - 授與動詞進階句型與代名詞受詞
【當受詞是代名詞 (it / them) 時的唯一結構】
✅ He gave it to me. (正確)
❌ He gave me it. (文法不自然 / 錯誤)
【即時隨堂測驗】
1. Linda made a delicious cake ______ her brother. (to / for)
2. Eric sent a postcard ______ his teacher. (to / for)
💡 答案解析：
1. for (make 為付費/製作類，用 for)
2. to (send 為方向傳遞類，用 to)

🎙️ Slide 6: C3 表達組 - 英語氣象主播情境表達
【情境短文 (Weather Report)】
"Good morning, Taipei! It is cold and rainy today. Don't forget to take an umbrella with you when you go out. My mother gave a warm jacket to me before I left home. Have a wonderful day!"
【書寫表達要點】
1. 正確使用氣候形容詞 (cold, rainy, snowy).
2. 授與動詞與介系詞的精準搭配 (gave ... to ... / bought ... for ...).
3. 首字大寫與句尾標點檢查。
💡 寫作評分指標：
1. 語法正確性 (動詞時態、介系詞 to/for 搭配)
2. 詞彙豐富度 (weather, umbrella, jacket, rainy, cloudy)
3. 標點與大小寫規範 (首字大寫、句尾句號)
