🎯 Unit 1 共同學習核心鎖定 (Common Core)

📌 本單元教學核心四柱：

  • Seasons (四季): spring (春), summer (夏), autumn/fall (秋), winter (冬)
  • Weather (+y 形容詞): sun ➔ sunny, rain ➔ rainy, cloud ➔ cloudy, snow ➔ snowy, wind ➔ windy
  • Weather Questions (天氣問答): How is the weather? = What is the weather like?
  • Ditransitive Verbs (授與雙賓動詞): give/send/write + to vs buy/make + for
💡 教學提點： 提醒學生名詞加 y 變成形容詞；授與動詞「人+物」與「物+to/for+人」的雙向對應關係。

🌸 Theme Words: Seasons & Weather

1. 季節與氣候特徵：

  • 🌸 spring: warm, rainy, flowers bloom
  • 🏖️ summer: hot, sunny, summer vacation
  • 🍁 autumn / fall: cool, windy
  • ⛄ winter: cold, snowy, snow in the mountains

2. 名詞 ➔ 形容詞規則 (+y)：

sun ➔ sunny (雙寫 n + y) ｜ rain ➔ rainy ｜ cloud ➔ cloudy ｜ snow ➔ snowy ｜ wind ➔ windy
💡 例句解析： It is sunny and hot today. (主詞 It 指天氣，後面放形容詞)。

☀️ Grammar Focus 1: 天氣問答與動名詞句型

句型一：問天氣

👉 How is the weather today?
= What is the weather like today? (注意有 like 要用 What！)

句型二：下雨與下雪的三種講法

  • 1. It rains a lot in summer. (動詞 rains)
  • 2. There is a lot of rain in summer. (名詞 rain)
  • 3. We have a lot of rain in summer. (名詞 rain)
💡 易錯提醒： How is the weather like? ❌ 錯誤！有 like (介系詞) 必須搭配疑問代名詞 What！

🎁 Grammar Focus 2: 授與雙賓動詞 (to vs for)

結構公式：

A 式： [主詞] + [動詞] + [人] + [物] ➔ Uncle gave me a card.
B 式： [主詞] + [動詞] + [物] + [to/for] + [人] ➔ Uncle gave a card to me.

介系詞分類口訣：

  • 👉 to (方向/直接傳遞): give, send, write, lend, show
  • 👉 for (付出心意/為某人做): buy, make
💡 實戰例題：
1. Kate bought a jacket for Pat.
2. Ariel sent a birthday gift to her cousin.

👦 Jamie: "How was your summer vacation, Zac?"

👦 Jamie: "I traveled to Australia. It was cold, and it snowed for a few days."
👦 Jamie: "I sent you a postcard from Australia. My parents also gave me a warm jacket!"

  • 🌱 C1 基礎組: 聚焦看圖認四季、5 大天氣形容詞二選一、give me a card 基礎圖卡，提供大量口語報讀與及時增強。
