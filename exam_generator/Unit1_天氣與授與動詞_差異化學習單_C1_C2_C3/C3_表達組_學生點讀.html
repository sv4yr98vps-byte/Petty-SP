一、授與動詞雙向句型改寫 (Ditransitive Verbs)

公式：[主詞] + [動詞] + [人] + [物] ➔ [主詞] + [動詞] + [物] + to / for + [人]

1. Kate bought Pat a jacket two days ago.
= Kate
標準答案：bought a jacket for Pat two days ago.
2. Ariel sent her cousin a birthday gift last week.
= Ariel
標準答案：sent a birthday gift to her cousin last week.
3. Uncle Sam made me a kite yesterday.
= Uncle Sam
標準答案：made a kite for me yesterday.
二、情境閱讀：跨國暑假與天氣
📖 點擊任一句子單獨聽讀，或點上方按鈕自動循序朗讀

During the summer vacation, Zac visited his aunt in India. The weather there was very hot, and it rained a lot.

At the same time, Jamie traveled to Australia. Because Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere, July and August are winter months. It was cold, and it snowed for a few days.

Jamie sent a postcard to Zac from Australia. His parents gave him a lovely warm jacket for his birthday.

1. Why was it winter in Australia during July and August? (為什麼 7、8 月澳洲是冬天？)
2. What did Jamie's parents give him for his birthday? (爸媽給了 Jamie 什麼生日禮物？)
