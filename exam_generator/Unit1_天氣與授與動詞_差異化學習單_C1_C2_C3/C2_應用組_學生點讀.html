🌉 教學橋樑一：名詞 ➔ 天氣形容詞 (+y)

形容詞用來描述天氣（例句：It is sunny. 天氣很晴朗；It is rainy. 天氣下著雨）。

名詞 (事物) 形容詞 (天氣狀態) 氣溫/特徵 例句 (點擊聽讀)
sun (太陽) sunny (晴朗的) hot (熱) It is sunny and hot in summer.
rain (雨) rainy (下雨的) wet (濕) It is rainy in spring.
cloud (雲) cloudy (多雲的) cool (涼爽) It is cloudy today.
snow (雪) snowy (下雪的) cold (寒冷) It is cold and snowy in winter.

🌉 教學橋樑二：授與動詞介系詞 (to vs for)
介系詞 代表動詞 口訣意義 例句 (點擊聽讀)
to give, send, write, lend, show 動作有「方向性」，直接交給/寄給對方 gave a postcard to me
for buy, make 含有「付出心意、為了某人」製作或購買 bought a jacket for Pat
二、授與動詞介系詞填空 (to / for)
1. ✉️ The man wrote a card his kid. (write a card 寫卡片「給」某人 ➔ 用 to)
2. 🧥 Kate bought a lovely jacket Pat. (buy a jacket 買夾克「為了」某人 ➔ 用 for)
3. 🪁 Uncle Sam made a kite me. (make a kite 做風箏「為了」某人 ➔ 用 for)
三、短對話閱讀：暑假旅遊與天氣
👦 Jamie: How was your summer vacation, Zac?
👦 Zac: It was good. I visited my aunt in India.
👦 Jamie: How was the weather there?
👦 Zac: It was hot, and it rained a lot.
👦 Jamie: I went to Australia. It was cold and snowy!
1. 請問 Zac 暑假去了哪裡？
2. 澳洲 (Australia) 的天氣如何？
