【C1 基礎組 參考答案】

一、生詞辨析： 1. (A)   2. (B)   3. (C)   4. (D)   5. (A)

二、文法填空： 6. (A)   7. (B)   8. (A)   9. (A)   10. (C)

【C2 應用組 參考答案】

1. I am interested in this food delivery app.

2. The video game is exciting to them.

3. The movie is boring and he is bored.

4. We believe that this new plan is very useful.

5. Tom told us that he wanted to go home.

6. Climbing all these stairs is very tiring.

【C3 表達組 參考答案與評分指引】

一、選擇題： 1. (A)   2. (C)   3. (C)   4. (B)   5. (A)

二、書寫題：

6. The video game is very exciting to them. (事為主詞用 exciting to them)

7. I know that she is interested in the plan. (that名詞子句 + interested in)

8. The delivery driver wears a clean uniform. (注意 delivery driver 與 uniform)
