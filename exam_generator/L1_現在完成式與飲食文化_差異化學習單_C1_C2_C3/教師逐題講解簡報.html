🎯 L1 共同學習核心鎖定 (Common Core)
【核心單字 1200 聚焦】
culture (文化), delicious (美味的), experience (經驗), popular (受歡迎的), traditional (傳統的), try (品嚐/嘗試), eat-ate-eaten (吃), see-saw-seen (看), visit (參訪), already (已經), yet (尚未), since (自從), for (長達)
【核心文法結構】
1. 現在完成式公式： 主詞 + have / has + 過去分詞 (p.p.)
2. 經驗用法： Have you ever eaten sushi? - Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
3. 持續用法 (since / for)：
  • since + 起點時間點 (e.g., since 2020, since yesterday)
  • for + 一段時間長度 (e.g., for three years, for two hours)

🍜 Slide 2: C1 基礎組 - 現在完成式公式
【助動詞 have / has 選擇依據】
• I / You / We / They + have + p.p.
• He / She / It / 單數主詞 + has + p.p.
【例句示範】
• I have eaten stinky tofu before. (我以前吃過臭豆腐。)
• She has lived in Tainan for five years. (她在台南住了五年。)
💡 教學引導： 否定句只要在助動詞後加 not：have not (haven't) / has not (hasn't)。

🔤 Slide 3: C1 基礎組 - 必背 1200 動詞三態 (p.p.)
• eat ➔ ate ➔ eaten
• see ➔ saw ➔ seen
• go ➔ went ➔ gone / been
• do ➔ did ➔ done
• write ➔ wrote ➔ written
• buy ➔ bought ➔ bought
💡 記憶技巧： 過去分詞 (p.p.) 絕不能單獨當主要動詞，前方必須有 have 或 has 輔助！

⏳ Slide 4: C2 應用組 - 時間副詞判斷 (since vs. for)
【判斷原則】
• since + 起點 (時間點/過去式子句)
  ➔ since 2018 / since last night / since I was a child
• for + 長度 (一段時間)
  ➔ for three days / for ten minutes / for a long time
【即時判斷】
1. We have known each other ______ ten years. (since / for)
2. Amy has studied English ______ 2021. (since / for)
💡 答案解析：
1. for (ten years 為一段時間長度)
2. since (2021 為明確起點時間點)

✈️ Slide 5: C2 應用組 - have been to vs. have gone to
【極高頻會考重點】
• have / has been to + 地點：去過某地 (人已回來，表人生經驗)
  • I have been to Japan twice. (我去過日本兩次。)

• have / has gone to + 地點：去某地了 (人還在當地或途中)
  • Tom is not here; he has gone to Japan. (湯姆不在，他去日本了。)
💡 陷阱提醒： 第一人稱 I 和第二人稱 You 正在對話，因此通常不能說 I have gone to... (除非在靈魂出竅或特殊語境)。

🌮 Slide 6: C3 表達組 - 異國美食經驗寫作
【寫作範文示例 (Food Culture & Experience)】
"Taiwan is famous for its delicious night market snacks. I have tried pearl milk tea and stinky tofu many times. My family has lived in Taipei for ten years, and we love exploring different food cultures together!"
【書寫表達三大指標】
1. 熟練使用現在完成式 (have tried / has lived).
2. 正確搭配時間副詞 (for ten years / since childhood / before).
3. 詞彙與拼字精準度。
💡 教學評量指引：
鼓勵學生以第一人稱 I have... 描述自己的真實生活經驗，強化語言與生活的連結。
