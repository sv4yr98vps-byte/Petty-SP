二、情境短文閱讀：國中開學第一天
📖 點擊任一句子單獨聽讀，或點上方按鈕自動循序朗讀（黃色光條跟隨）

Today is the first day of junior high school. Mike meets Tina in the classroom.

Mike: "Hello! I am Mike. What is your name?"

Tina: "Hi, Mike! My name is Tina. This is my dog. Its name is Rocky."

Mike: "Nice to meet you, Tina! Rocky is so cute. The ball is for it."

Tina: "Thank you! What's your phone number?"

Mike: "It is 2345-0189. Let's practice English together!"

1. Who is Rocky? (Rocky 是誰？)
2. What is the ball for? (這顆球是給誰的？)
3. What is Mike's phone number? (Mike 的電話號碼是？)
三、專業英文書寫校對員 (Proofreading)

1. how are you? i am fine.thank you (有 4 處錯誤)
