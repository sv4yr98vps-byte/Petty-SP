一、現在完成式句型改寫 (Present Perfect Transformation)

請依題意將過去動作改寫為現在完成式（注意主詞單複數與動詞 p.p.）：

1. Jay and Cate became friends in 2000. (They are still friends now.)
= Jay and Cate
標準答案：have been friends since 2000.
2. Rick started working in the library a month ago.
= Rick
標準答案：has worked in the library for a month.
3. Have you ever made candy apples before? (以肯定的完整句回答)
=
標準答案：Yes, I have made candy apples before.
二、情境長文閱讀：Are French Fries Truly French?
📖 點擊任一句子單獨聽讀，或點上方按鈕自動循序朗讀

The French gave the world the first hairdryer, paper machine, and bikini. However, they didn't give the world French fries. Many people probably haven't heard about this fact yet.

The earliest French fries were from Belgium along the Meuse River in the late 17th century. When the river froze in winter, people could not catch fish for food. So they would cut potatoes into long thin sticks and fry them.

During World War I, American soldiers in Belgium tasted this delicious food. They called these fried potatoes "French fries" because French was the main language there. Since then, French fries have become popular all over the world.

1. What is the reading mainly about? (這篇文章主要在說明什麼？)
2. Why did American soldiers call them "French fries"? (為什麼美軍稱它們為 French fries？)
