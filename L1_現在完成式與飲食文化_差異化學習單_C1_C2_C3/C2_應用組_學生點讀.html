🌉 教學橋樑一：現在完成式結構 (have/has + p.p.)

現在完成式用來表達「人生經驗」或「從過去持續到現在的動作」。主詞為第三人稱單數 (he/she) 用 has，其餘用 have。

原形動詞 過去式 過去分詞 (p.p.) 例句 (點擊聽讀)
eat (吃) ate eaten Have you ever eaten candy apples?
make (做) made made I have made chocolate eggs before.
see (看見) saw seen Have you ever seen snow?
bake (烘烤) baked baked She has baked pumpkin pies.

🌉 教學橋樑二：持續時間介系詞 (for vs since)
介系詞 後接時間類型 常見搭配範例 例句 (點擊聽讀)
for 一段持續的時間 (長度) for two hours, for a month, for ten years has worked here for a month
since 某個過去的起點 (時間點/子句) since 2000, since last year, since she was 10 have been friends since 2000
二、時間介系詞填空 (for / since)
1. 📚 Rick has worked in the library a month. (a month 是一整個月的「時間長度」 ➔ 用 for)
2. 🤝 Jay and Cate have been friends 2000. (2000 年是過去的一個「時間起點」 ➔ 用 since)
3. 📺 They have watched TV two hours. (two hours 是兩小時的「時間長度」 ➔ 用 for)
