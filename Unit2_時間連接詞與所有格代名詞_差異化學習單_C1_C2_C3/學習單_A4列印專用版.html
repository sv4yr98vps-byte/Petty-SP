依提示字詞重組句子（每題 10 分，共 100 分）
1. 【Before / Miss Turner / went to work, / she / bought / a sandwich.】（在特納小姐上班前...）
2. 【Miss Turner / bought / a sandwich / before / she / went to work.】（特納小姐上班前買了...）
3. 【After / Yuki / talked / to her cousin, / she / joined / the club.】（在 Yuki 與堂哥談過後...）
4. 【Yuki / joined / the club / after / she / talked / to her cousin.】（Yuki 談過後加入了社團...）
5. 【When / Dylan / was / at the airport, / he / called / his parents.】（當 Dylan 在機場時...）
6. 【Dylan / called / his parents / when / he / was / at the airport.】（Dylan 在機場時打給父母...）
7. 【This sandwich / is / mine, / not / yours.】（這份三明治是我的，不是你的。）
8. 【This car / is / not his; / it / is / hers.】（這輛車不是他的；它是她的。）
9. 【These jackets / are / theirs, / and ours / are / over there.】（這些外套是他們的，而我們的是...）
10. 【Whose camera / is this / on the table? / It / is / mine.】（桌上這台相機是誰的？它是我的。）

【C1 基礎組 參考答案與精簡解析】
1. (A) club (water sports club 水上運動社團)
2. (B) interesting (電腦科學很有趣)
3. (B) hold on (電話用語：請稍候)
4. (A) wrong (wrong number 撥錯電話)
5. (A) Whose (疑問詞：誰的)
6. (B) science (computer science 電腦科學)
7. (C) told (tell 的過去式，句型 tell sb about sth)
8. (C) poor (poor swimmer 拙劣的泳者)
9. (A) mine (所有格代名詞 = my sandwich)
10. (B) hers (= that girl's car)
11. (B) theirs (= their jackets)
12. (A) those girls' (複數名詞所有格只加撇號)
13. (A) Before (在...之前)
14. (A) after (在...之後)
15. (A) When (當...的時候)
