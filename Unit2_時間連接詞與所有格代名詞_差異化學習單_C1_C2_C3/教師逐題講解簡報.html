SLIDE 3 / 10 · PHONE CONVERSATION & IDIOMS 實用生活溝通英語

📞 電話情境實戰與必考成語

📞 5 大標準電話應答句型

• 1. 找某人通話：
May I speak to Yuki, please?

• 2. 本人接聽（我就是）：
Speaking. / This is Yuki speaking.
⚠️ 嚴禁說 I am Yuki. 這是中文直譯錯誤！

• 3. 請對方稍候（別掛斷）：
Please hold on. / Hold on a second.

• 4. 打錯電話：
You have the wrong number.

• 5. 不在請回電：
She's not in. Please call back later.

💡 2 大日常高頻必考成語

1. pull one's leg（開某人玩笑、唬弄某人）
• Are you pulling my leg? You don't know how to swim!
👉 意同：Are you joking with me?
2. make up one's mind（下定決心、做出決定）
• Dylan made up his mind to sign up for the science club.
👉 意同：decide / make a decision.
🎯 會考常考題眼： 題目問 "May I speak to Mr. Lin?"，若接聽者正是 Mr. Lin，正解必定是 "Speaking."！
SLIDE 4 / 10 · POSSESSIVE PRONOUNS MATRIX 文法核心突破

👜 所有格代名詞 (Possessive Pronouns) 全矩陣

公式：所有格代名詞 (Pronoun) ＝ 所有格 (Adj) ＋ 名詞 (Noun)（功能：避免重複出現名詞贅字）

人稱 / 主格 (S) 所有格 (Possessive Adj) 所有格代名詞 (Pronoun) 替換前 ➔ 替換後（精簡例句）
第一人稱單數 I my mine This is my sandwich. ➔ This sandwich is mine.
第一人稱複數 we our ours These are our jackets. ➔ These jackets are ours.
第二人稱 you your yours Is that your camera? ➔ Is that camera yours?
第三人稱男 he his his That is his umbrella. ➔ That umbrella is his.
第三人稱女 she her hers It is her car. ➔ The car is hers.
第三人稱複數 they their theirs Those are their bags. ➔ Those bags are theirs.
複數名詞 those girls those girls' those girls' The balls are those girls' balls. ➔ They are those girls'.
🔑 口訣記憶：
1. mine 獨特無 s；his 本身帶 s 不變。
2. 其餘（yours, hers, ours, theirs）皆在所有格後加上 -s。
3. Whose + N is this?（這是誰的相機？）➔ It's mine / hers / Jamie's.
SLIDE 5 / 10 · TIME CONJUNCTIONS 時間副詞子句

⏳ 時間連接詞三大巨頭 (When / Before / After) 句構精析

規則 1：連接詞放【句首】（子句後加逗號）

• Before S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
Before Miss Turner went to work, she bought a sandwich.

• After S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
After Alan got up, he made the bed.

• When S1 + V1, S2 + V2.
When Dylan was at the airport, he called his parents.

規則 2：連接詞放【句中】（不加逗號）

• S2 + V2 + before + S1 + V1.
Miss Turner bought a sandwich before she went to work.

• S2 + V2 + after + S1 + V1.
Alan made the bed after he got up.

• S2 + V2 + when + S1 + V1.
Dylan called his parents when he was at the airport.
⚠️ 時態三大黃金律：
1. 過去事件時態呼應： 敘述過去發生的動作，兩邊動詞皆使用過去式（went, bought / got, made / was, called）。
2. 普遍規律與習慣： 敘述習慣真理，兩邊動詞皆使用現在式（When the weather is hot, the kids eat ice cream.）。

• Q1 (club): water sports [ c _ _ b ] ➔ 社團。
• Q2 (interesting): very [ i _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ g ] ➔ 有趣的。
• Q3 (hold on): 請在線上稍候 ➔ Please [ ______ ].
• Q4 (leg): pull one's [ l _ g ] ➔ 開玩笑/唬人。
• Q5 (mind): make up one's [ m _ _ d ] ➔ 下定決心。
• Q6 (science): computer [ s _ _ _ _ _ e ] ➔ 電腦科學。
• Q7 (poor): a [ p _ _ r ] swimmer ➔ 拙劣的泳者。
• Q8 (whose): [ W _ _ _ e ] camera is this? ➔ 誰的。

Part 2: 文法句型 7 題重點突破 (3選1)

• Q9 (mine): This sandwich is [ my sandwich ] ➔ mine.
• Q10 (hers): That car is [ her car ] ➔ hers.
• Q11 (theirs): These jackets are [ their jackets ] ➔ theirs.
• Q12 (those girls'): 複數所有格代名詞 ➔ those girls'.

SLIDE 7 / 10 · C2 UNSCRAMBLE BREAKTHROUGH 應用組 10 題精講

🚀 C2 應用組 核心句型重組與易錯點突破 (共 10 題)

組句 1-5：時間副詞子句語序翻轉

• Q1: Before Dylan goes to school, he usually eats breakfast.
• Q2: Dylan usually eats breakfast before he goes to school.
• Q3: After we finished our homework, we watched TV.
• Q4: We watched TV after we finished our homework.
• Q5: When Dylan was at the airport, he called his parents.

組句 6-10：代名詞代換與雙重所有格

• Q6: Dylan called his parents when he was at the airport.
• Q7: This sandwich is mine, not yours.
• Q8: This car is not his; it is hers.
• Q9: These jackets are theirs, and ours are over there.
• Q10: Whose camera is this on the table? It is mine.
🔑 教學指導技巧：
1. 逗號檢查法： 引導學生先找出連接詞（When/Before/After）。若連接詞排在最前面，則第一段動作結束時必須緊跟逗號（,）。
2. 代名詞檢驗法： 空格後無名詞時，必須選用所有格代名詞（mine, yours, hers, theirs）。

SLIDE 9 / 10 · EXAM TRAPS & TIPS 會考高頻陷阱

💡 歷屆會考題型延伸與解題大補帖

⚠️ 陷阱 1：his 的雙重身分

• This is his jacket.（his 是所有格形容詞）
• This jacket is his.（his 是所有格代名詞）
👉 提醒： his 兩者拼字完全相同，千萬不要寫成 hiss！

⚠️ 陷阱 2：複數名詞所有格撇號

• the girl's（一個女孩的）
• the girls'（多個女孩的）
• the children's（孩子們的，不規則複數需加 's）
👉 技巧： 字尾已經有 s 的複數，只要在最後面加撇號 ' 即可。

⚠️ 陷阱 3：連接詞子句與主要子句

• Before she went to work, she bought a sandwich.
• She bought a sandwich before she went to work.
👉 重點： 無論順序如何變換，before 引導的永遠是「動作先發生的條件背景」！
🎓 考試秒殺技巧： 看到句子末尾沒有名詞，且前面提過物品（jacket/camera/sandwich），直接在選項中鎖定帶有 -s 的所有格代名詞（mine, yours, hers, theirs, ours）！
